=====================================================================
IMPORTANT FRAMING (read first)
=====================================================================
Lottery draws are independent random events. Every number from 00 to 99
has exactly the same probability on every single draw (1 in 100 for a
straight quiniela position). Past frequency does NOT change future odds.
"Hot" and "cold" numbers are descriptions of the past, not predictions of
the future. This dossier is for entertainment and pattern-exploration
only. Treat any "predicted" number as a guess with no real edge.

SIMULATION GOAL FOR THE AGENTS
=====================================================================
Using the historical patterns above, have the agent population reason
about and propose candidates for the next draws of:
  1. QUINIELA (Leidsa & Nacional) — three numbers 00–99
  2. LOTO POOL — a set from balls 01–31
  3. LOTO MÁS — six numbers from 01–40 plus a Más ball 01–12
  4. SUPER KINO TV — a spread from balls 01–80

Agents should debate hot-number momentum, cold-number "due" theories,
positional tendencies (e.g. quiniela 84 favoring 1st position),
digit-family clustering, and balanced vs. clustered ball spreads — then
converge on a small set of candidate predictions per game WITH explicit
confidence levels and the honest caveat that true odds are uniform and
no strategy improves the real probability of winning.
